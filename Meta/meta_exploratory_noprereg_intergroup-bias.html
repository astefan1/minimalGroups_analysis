Intergroup Bias

Angelika Stefan

2026-10-30

In the following, we present exploratory, non-preregistered analyses of intergroup bias. Intergroup bias is the outcome variable in all analyses, and we assume that, typically, participants should prefer their ingroup over the outgroup. Here, we test whether the intergroup bias is present in all studies.
